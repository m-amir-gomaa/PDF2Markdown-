What the service provides

Pageweft converts PDFs you are authorized to process into Markdown and associated files. You can inspect a PDF, select pages, review extraction against the source, make corrections and download an archive. Maximum upload: 64 MiB and 1,000 pages. Scans, equations, tables, pronunciation symbols and complex layouts can require manual correction. The service does not guarantee perfect transcription, learning outcomes or professional accuracy.
